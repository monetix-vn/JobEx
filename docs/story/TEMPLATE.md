# Series bible: <Job title>

Status: draft | approved by owner (date). Read `content/industry-cookware/events/README.md` for tone.
Scenes may only be written against an approved bible and its episode list.

## 1. Premise (3 sentences)

Who is the player, what company, what is this job really about, and what makes it hard?

## 2. What the player should learn

The 3 to 5 true things about this job a player should feel by the end (not slogans: situations).

## 3. The player's arc over the year

Start (new, eager) -> middle (pressure, first compromise) -> end (who did they become?). Three or
four beats, and how the choices bend it.

## 4. Cast (about 5 recurring characters)

| Character | Title / dept | Wants | Fears | Flaw or tic | Relationship arc (how they change) |
| --------- | ------------ | ----- | ----- | ----------- | ---------------------------------- |

Include: one mentor-or-boss, one rival or friction, one ally, one outsider (buyer, auditor,
supplier), one wildcard.

## 5. Season timeline (by month)

| Month | Business reality (peak, close, audit, Tet) | Beat / storyline starting or peaking |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |

Fixed beats: premiere (week 1-2), midseason twist (~week 26), crisis (~week 40), finale (~week 50).

## 6. Storylines (arcs)

For each: name, who, stages (1, 2, 3...), branches by choice, how it ends, facts it creates.

## 7. The dark-side ladder

From tiny to serious, the temptations this job offers, in order. Each rung: the deed, why it is
tempting, what it costs later, the fact id.

## 8. Endings

Completed, burnout, fired, prosecuted, promoted, walked away, and what each looks like in this job.
