# 0005: Content boundaries for the dark path

Status: accepted on 2026-10-02 (owner: "ok" to section 2 of `docs/design/WORLD-PLAN.md`).
The owner wants no fixed limit on how dark a path can get, so the world can mimic real life.

## Decision

1. Crime and violence (including blackmail, bribery, coercion, exploitation, theft, assault, killing) are
   modelled as **causes and consequences** in the simulation, with no scripted ceiling.
2. Presentation is **non-graphic and non-glorifying**. Sexual violence, sexual coercion and prostitution exist
   only as **abstract, off-screen events with their consequences** (investigation, trauma, justice, social
   effects). No explicit or titillating depiction is written. Nothing sexual involving minors is modelled in
   any form.
3. Real historical or living people are not used; extreme careers are emergent from the player's choices.
4. An **intensity setting** at world creation controls how explicit text may be and which themes are skipped;
   the underlying simulation does not change.
5. The point of the systems is to show how people and institutions produce harm and what it costs. Tone rules
   in `content/industry-cookware/events/README.md` apply. Dark-path content and the culture pack get
   specialist review (law, criminology, sensitivity, Vietnamese culture) before release.

## Consequences

- Some content the owner may imagine (explicit scenes) will not be written; consequences, justice and
  aftermath will be.
